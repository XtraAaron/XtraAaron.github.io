Transforming Home Assistance Robot

SolidWorks Mechanism Design GD&T

Description

The Transforming Home Assistance Robot is a robot design to assist the elderly in home tasks. It is designed to transform for easy storage, and fit into a dock. The robot arm fits a interchangable end effector that can be a claw arm for grabbing objects, or a tablet holder.
